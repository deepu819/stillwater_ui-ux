Stillwater Helplines

Someone is here

You don't have to explain it well.

This is a quiet text window. No name, no form, no questions you must answer. Type as little as you want, or nothing at all.

Start quietly Just show me someone to call
  • Nothing is requiredYou can pause, leave, or delete this conversation at any moment.
  • No name, no accountYou get a random word-name for today. Nothing links back to you.
  • No judgementThere is no correct way to use this, and no wrong thing to say.
What happens to what I write?
  • Your words are not stored by default. Only the fact that someone spoke to us today is counted.
  • If you turn on keep my words inside the chat, then and only then are your sentences saved - and you can switch it off again.
  • No cookies, so nothing follows you to other sites. Closing the tab ends the conversation.
  • We never ask for your name, phone number, email or location, and we do not log your IP address.
  • The chat is a simple rule-based guide, not a person and not an AI model. A trained human is always one tap away.
